# progressive routing

Resolution as the composite of per-hop responsibilities: each intermediate owns only its next hop, so re-binding one intermediate re-routes everything through it.

Defining segment: [[def-locations-and-paths]]. This entry is the lexicon projection; the segment owns the full treatment — on divergence, the segment wins and this entry updates.
